Sales Partners

Your dedicated real estate resource.

Finding the right home is about more than choosing a property. That’s why O’Grady Development has selected a small group of real estate professionals to serve our buyers, and why every O’Grady Development inquiry is paired with one of them: an O’Grady Development Real Estate Specialist from LiveMichigan.

Your Sales Partner serves as your real estate agent and resource throughout Northern Michigan, helping you explore O’Grady Development homes and communities, evaluate other properties across the region, understand your options, and navigate the process from your first conversation through closing. They can also connect you with trusted local lenders, insurance professionals, inspectors, and other resources along the way.
