The Road Is In.

i. Privacy & Space

Room to Spread Out.

With just 17 homesites ranging from approximately 2.5 to 3 acres, Westacres offers room to spread out without sacrificing the convenience of being close to Traverse City.

Generously sized properties, approximately 200 feet wide and 600 feet deep, provide space between neighbors and the opportunity to create a home that feels distinctly your own. Whether that means expansive outdoor living areas, gardens, natural landscaping, or simply a little more peace and quiet, Westacres offers the setting to enjoy it.

ii. Freedom to Make It Your Own

Built Around the Way
You Want to Live.

We recognize that everyone has a different vision for their property. Some envision a custom-built home surrounded by beautifully maintained grounds. Others want space for a detached garage, workshop, recreational equipment, or hobby farming.

Westacres embraces that individuality.

Rather than prescribing a specific lifestyle, the community is designed to accommodate a variety of interests and property uses. It's an opportunity to create a home and surrounding property that complement the way you want to live.

iii. Thoughtful Standards

Thoughtful Standards.
Lasting Value.

Freedom and quality can coexist.

Westacres incorporates reasonable architectural and property maintenance standards intended to preserve the appearance, character, and long-term desirability of the community.

Our approach is straightforward: establish clear expectations for quality construction and responsible property ownership while respecting the independence that makes acreage living so appealing.

These standards are designed to protect the community without imposing unnecessary restrictions on how homeowners enjoy their land.

At Westacres, you don't have to choose between the freedom of rural living and the confidence of a thoughtfully planned neighborhood.
